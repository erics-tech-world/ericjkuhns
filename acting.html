My Acting History

I first played a U.S. patriot in a kindergarten play and ever since, I’ve been obsessed with acting and film. There’s something both mythical and natural about stepping into someone else’s shoes and taking on their personality and mannerisms.

After high school, I started filming skits with friends and decided to pursue acting more seriously in 2010. I entered a 15-month Acting Performance program and earned my associate degree in 2012. Soon after, I joined the Kim Dawson Agency in Dallas and worked through them from 2012 to 2016.

Since then, I’ve continued acting through independent projects, commercials, industrials, documentaries, skits and my own videos. I’m interested in both the controlled process of film and the immediacy of live performance.

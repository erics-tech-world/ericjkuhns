Spacecosis

A sci-fi novel by Eric J. Kuhns

Into the cosmos we go, in search of a new home.

[ Transmission Start: Channel 7.391.044.∞ ]

Good evening, and thank you for tuning in. I've got a strange one for you tonight. Might be the strangest I'll ever tell.

It starts with Dr. Natalia Zeal, who is brilliant, stubborn, and one of the most amazing human beings I've ever known. She left her dying home planet, Earth, on a ship built to outrun extinction, carrying her frozen consciousness, her life's work, and a photo of the woman she loved tucked somewhere close. She wakes up eons later, in a body that isn't hers and on a strange planet that isn't either. Someone, or something, has been watching. This is where our story truly begins...

I won't tell you everything that happens next. I'll just say it's stranger than you're expecting, and it isn't only her story to tell. It's mine too, in ways I'm still working out even as I broadcast this.
